What we help with

Seven signs worth a conversation.

None of these mean something is wrong with you — they mean it might be time to talk to someone. If any of this sounds familiar, that's reason enough to reach out.

Anxiety

A mind that won't sit still — constant worry, racing thoughts, and restlessness that doesn't ease with rest.

Anger issues

Short fuses and sudden outbursts — irritation that flares before you can catch it, and a feeling of losing control.

Depression

A heaviness that lingers — persistent sadness, hopelessness, and losing interest in things you used to enjoy.

Lack of motivation

Feeling stuck in place — no drive or desire to do anything, even things that used to feel easy.

Lack of concentration

Thoughts that scatter easily — difficulty focusing, tasks left half-finished, small things slipping your mind.

Insomnia

Nights spent staring at the ceiling — trouble falling asleep, waking often, tired no matter how long you lie there.

Phobias

A sharp, outsized fear of certain places, objects, or situations that others move through without a second thought.

The Ayurvedic view

Why the mind is treated like the body.

Ayurveda reads emotional patterns as signs of imbalance, not flaws — three underlying forces, or doshas, that shape how each person's mind tends to drift under stress.

Vata

Movement & the nervous system

Vata governs motion and the nervous system. When it runs high, it often shows up as anxiety, racing thoughts, and restless, broken sleep.

Pitta

Heat & transformation

Pitta governs body heat and drive. An aggravated Pitta is classically linked to irritability, sharp anger, and a mind that runs too hot for too long.

Kapha

Stability & structure

Kapha governs stability and structure. When it grows heavy or stagnant, it can flatten mood, motivation, and mental clarity.

Good to know

Questions people usually ask.

Is this a replacement for psychiatric treatment?+

No. Smrithi Counselling works alongside, not instead of, medical or psychiatric care. If you're already seeing a psychiatrist or are in crisis, please continue that care and reach out to emergency services first.

Is everything I share confidential?+

Yes. Sessions are one-on-one and private. Nothing discussed is shared without your consent.

Can I join from outside Kerala, or from abroad?+

Yes. Sessions run over Google Meet, so you can join from anywhere with a stable internet connection.

How do I prepare for my first session?+

You don't need to prepare anything. Come as you are — the first session is mainly about listening and understanding your situation.

How soon can I get an appointment?+

Call or email us directly and we'll find the next available slot for a two-hour session.
